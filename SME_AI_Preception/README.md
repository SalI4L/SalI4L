# SME Employees’ Perception of AI in Creative Fields

This project explores how employees of **Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises (SMEs)** perceive the integration of **third-party AI tools** in creative industries, and how these perceptions impact **job performance and satisfaction**.  

The research was conducted as part of an academic project and includes **quantitative survey data, qualitative analysis, and supporting research papers**. The outcomes provide both an academic and applied perspective on AI adoption in SMEs.  

---

## 📂 Project Contents  

- **AI_in_creative_fields_Research_paper.pdf**  
  A full academic paper detailing the study design, methodology, results, and discussion.  

- **DataScienceAI2_Research_Proposal.pdf**  
  The initial research proposal outlining the research question, motivation, and expected outcomes.  

- **EDA-surveyData.ipynb**  
  A Jupyter Notebook with **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** of the collected survey data, including descriptive statistics and visualizations.  

- **Policy_paper_final.pdf**  
  A policy-oriented paper suggesting implications of AI adoption for SMEs and recommendations for stakeholders.  

- **Qualitative Data Analysis of AI Perceptions Among Creative Professionals.pdf**  
  A qualitative study focusing on thematic analysis of interview responses from creative professionals working with AI.  

- **Scientific poster.pdf**  
  A visual research poster summarizing the project, suitable for academic conferences or showcases.  

- **Stakeholder Analysis.pdf**  
  An assessment of key stakeholders involved in AI adoption in SMEs and their potential roles, benefits, and concerns.  

- **Survey questions.pdf**  
  The survey instrument used to collect data from SME employees, focusing on AI familiarity, job satisfaction, concerns, and perceived risks.  

---

## 🧑‍💻 Methods  

- **Quantitative Analysis**:  
  Survey responses converted into numerical data for statistical exploration.  

- **Qualitative Analysis**:  
  Thematic analysis of interviews to capture nuanced perceptions of AI in creative contexts.  

- **Mixed Approach**:  
  The project combines qualitative insights with quantitative trends to deliver a balanced perspective.  

---

## 🚀 Key Outcomes  

- Employees in creative SMEs express both **optimism** (AI as a tool to boost productivity and creativity) and **concern** (job risk, skill displacement).  
- Job satisfaction correlates with **comfort and familiarity** with AI tools.  
- Recommendations for SMEs and policymakers to encourage **responsible AI adoption**.

## ⚙️ How to Run  

To explore the data analysis part of this project:  

1. Clone or download this repository.  
2. Open the `EDA-surveyData.ipynb` file using **Jupyter Notebook** or **JupyterLab**.  
   ```bash
   jupyter notebook EDA-surveyData.ipynb
   ```  
3. Ensure you have the required Python libraries installed (e.g., `pandas`, `matplotlib`, `seaborn`).  
   ```bash
   pip install pandas matplotlib seaborn
   ```  
4. Run the notebook cells to reproduce the exploratory data analysis and visualizations.
